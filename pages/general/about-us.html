About Company

YS STUDY FOCUS is a Malaysia-focused education consultancy committed to helping Bangladeshi students make informed and confident decisions about their higher education journey.

We provide reliable information and personalized guidance on university and course selection, admissions, tuition fees, scholarships, documentation, and student visa processing. Our recommendations are tailored to each student's academic profile, budget, career goals, and future aspirations.

Our support extends beyond admission. From pre-departure preparation to post-arrival assistance in Malaysia, we remain a trusted partner throughout every step of the student journey.

Vision

To become a trusted and student-first education consultancy for Malaysia, recognized for honest counselling, reliable information, and genuine commitment to students' academic and career success.

Mission

Our mission is to provide students with accurate information, transparent guidance, and personalized counselling so they can make informed decisions about their education and future.

We aim to support our students at every stage from choosing the right university and program to completing admission and visa procedures, preparing for departure, and settling into their new academic journey in Malaysia.

Our Services

Our services are designed to help students make informed decisions based on their academic qualifications, preferred study programs, budget, and future career goals.

  • Free Study Consultation
  • University & Course Selection
  • Admission Application Support
  • Scholarship Guidance
  • Student Visa Processing
  • Document Preparation & Checking
  • Application & Placement Support
  • Pre-Departure Guidance
  • Airport Pickup Assistance
  • Post-Arrival Student Support
